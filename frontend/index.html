The Rupture Paradox: Why 65% of Fatal Heart Attacks Occur at <50% Stenosis

The Conventional Blindspot

Standard luminography (angiography) only visualizes the inside hollow pipe. A lesion causing only 30% or 40% narrowing is labeled "mild" and sent home with basic aspirin.

The Biophysical Reality

Soft lipid-rich plaques undergo outward positive remodeling (Glagov remodeling). They have thin fibrous caps (<65 µm) packed with macrophages. When located near arterial bifurcations, oscillating wall shear stress tears the fragile cap, triggering acute occlusive thrombosis in seconds.

What is CardioGraph AI & Why Does It Matter?

🌟 The Big Problem We Solve

Cardiovascular disease is the #1 killer on Earth (18 million lives per year). In typical hospital care, doctors order an angiogram and look for 70% or 80% blockages. If your blockage is only 30% or 40%, you are usually told: "You look fine, take a baby aspirin."

⚡

The Deadly "Rupture Paradox"

Over 65% of sudden fatal heart attacks occur at plaques with LESS than 50% blockage. These are "soft, lipid-rich vulnerable plaques" with paper-thin caps (<65 microns). When blood rushing through a turbulent arterial fork exerts shear stress, that thin cap tears open in seconds—triggering the massive "Widow Maker" heart attack.
